# Made for More brand guide (first version)

## Brand idea

**Made for More = partnerships that deliver more.**
A good match between a brand and a creator achieves more than either could alone. The **plus sign (+)** is the central brand mark: brand + creator.

- **Promise:** brands and creators who truly belong together.
- **Core values:** personal · well-matched · honest · transparent

## Logo (concept)

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `site/assets/img/logo.svg` | Primary logo on light backgrounds |
| `site/assets/img/logo-light.svg` | Logo on dark backgrounds |
| `site/assets/img/logo-mark.svg` / `site/favicon.svg` | Mark on its own: favicon, social media avatar, app icon |

**Construction:** a rounded dark tile with a white geometric **"m"** and an orange **"+"** at the top right. Next to it the wordmark **made for more+** in lowercase (Bricolage Grotesque, extra bold) with an orange plus.

**Rules**
- Minimum clear space around the logo: the height of the "+".
- Don't use the mark smaller than 24 px (digital) or 8 mm (print).
- Don't stretch, rotate or recolour it outside the palette.
- The "+" is always signal orange.

> **Note:** this is a first concept. In `logo.svg` the wordmark is still live text. Have the final logo converted to outlines in Figma or Illustrator so it renders identically everywhere. Consider checking trademark availability with the BOIP (Benelux Office for Intellectual Property).

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0F1115` | Main text, dark surfaces, buttons |
| Paper | `#F7F5F0` | Main background (warm off-white) |
| Paper 2 | `#EFEBE3` | Alternating sections |
| Signal orange | `#FF5A36` | Accent: the "+", call-to-action buttons, highlights. Use on dark or as a fill, not for small text on light |
| Orange text | `#B83316` | Orange text on light backgrounds (contrast 5.4:1) |
| Grey | `#565B65` | Secondary text on light (6.3:1) |
| Light grey | `#A9AEB8` | Secondary text on dark (7.6:1) |
| Line | `#DDD7CB` | Borders and dividers |

Ratio: roughly 70% paper, 25% ink, 5% orange. The orange works because it is used sparingly.

## Typography

- **Headings:** [Bricolage Grotesque](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bricolage+Grotesque), 650–800, tight letter spacing (−2 to −3.5%). Characterful, contemporary, confident.
- **Body text:** [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter), 400–600. Highly readable, neutral and professional.
- Base size 17 px, line height 1.6. Headings scale fluidly with the screen.

## Tone of voice

**We sound like:** an honest, knowledgeable partner who stands beside brands and creators.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Direct and warm, address the reader as "you" | Distant, corporate language |
| Concrete: what we do, how and when | Vague: "innovative tailored solutions" |
| Short sentences, active voice | Long sentences, jargon |
| Honest: "you don't need that" | Selling everything |

**Example lines**
- "Brands and creators who truly belong together."
- "A good match is about more than follower count."
- "Let's make something great."

## Imagery (advice)

- Your own real photos at work, preferably no stock photos.
- Images of real partnerships (with permission from the brand and the creator).
- The "+" as a recurring graphic element on social media and in presentations.
