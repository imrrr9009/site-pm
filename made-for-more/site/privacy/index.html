Privacy statement

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Made for More respects your privacy. This statement explains which personal data we process, why, and what rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who we are

Made for More is an influencer sponsorship agency based in the Netherlands, run by Imran El Mayouri. We are responsible for the processing described here.

  • Email: madeformorebureau@outlook.com
  • Phone and WhatsApp: +31 6 44767638

Which data we process, and why

When you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we process your name, your contact details and the content of your message. We use this to reply to you and to prepare or carry out a collaboration. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into an agreement, or our legitimate interest in answering your question.

When you visit this website, our hosting provider automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit in server logs. This is needed to deliver the website and keep it secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest.

Cookies, only with your permission. If you click "Accept" in the cookie banner, we load a tracking script from our partner impact.com. It places cookies that record via which partner link you arrived on our site, so referrals can be attributed correctly. The legal basis is your consent. If you click "Decline", the script is not loaded and no tracking cookies are placed. We remember your choice in your browser's local storage.

Changing your cookie choice

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time.

Who we share data with

We never sell your data. We only share it with parties we need to run our business:

  • our hosting provider, which delivers this website;
  • Microsoft (Outlook), for email;
  • Meta (WhatsApp), if you contact us via WhatsApp;
  • impact.com, only if you accept cookies.

Some of these parties are based outside the European Union, for example in the United States. Where that applies, they use safeguards recognised by the EU, such as standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep data

Messages that do not lead to a collaboration are deleted within 12 months of our last contact. Data of clients and creators we work with is kept for as long as the collaboration requires, and invoices for 7 years because of Dutch tax law.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Email us at madeformorebureau@outlook.com and we will respond within one month.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

We may update this statement. The latest version is always on this page.
